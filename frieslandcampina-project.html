GDPR Compliance & GA4 Migration

Google Analytics 4 GDPR Compliance Privacy Framework Global Implementation

Project Overview

As Technical Web Analytics Consultant for FrieslandCampina, I led a comprehensive initiative to ensure GDPR compliance across their global digital ecosystem while simultaneously preparing them for the transition to Google Analytics 4.

This dual-focus project required balancing technical implementation with strategic privacy considerations to create a framework that would support both current compliance requirements and future analytics capabilities.

Project Details

  • Timeline:
    April 2022 - February 2023
  • Role:
    Technical Web Analytics Consultant
  • Scope:
    Global websites and digital properties
  • Tools:
    Google Analytics 4, Google Tag Manager, Consent Management Platforms

Key Achievements

GDPR Compliance Implementation

Implemented comprehensive GDPR-compliant privacy frameworks across all FrieslandCampina websites, ensuring proper data collection practices and user consent mechanisms.

GA4 Migration Strategy

Designed and implemented a global Google Analytics 4 setup that prepared all websites for the transition from Universal Analytics while maintaining data continuity.

Global Standardization

Created standardized implementation templates and processes that enabled consistent tracking across different markets and brands within the FrieslandCampina portfolio.

90% Implementation Rate

Successfully achieved implementation of privacy frameworks and GA4 setups across 90% of all FrieslandCampina websites, setting a new standard for their digital analytics ecosystem.

The Challenge

FrieslandCampina faced significant challenges in their digital analytics landscape:

  • Diverse digital ecosystem with numerous websites across different markets
  • Inconsistent tracking implementations and consent management approaches
  • Approaching deadline for Universal Analytics sunset
  • Need for GDPR compliance while maintaining analytics capabilities
  • Various stakeholders with different levels of technical understanding

The project required balancing technical implementation with privacy compliance across a complex global organization.

The Solution

I developed a comprehensive approach to address these challenges:

  • Created a standardized privacy framework template adaptable to regional requirements
  • Designed a global GA4 implementation that captured essential business metrics
  • Developed clear documentation and implementation guidelines for all markets
  • Built a validation process to ensure proper compliance and data collection
  • Provided training and support to digital teams across the organization

This integrated approach ensured both technical success and organizational adoption.
