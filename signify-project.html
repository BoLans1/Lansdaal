The Challenge

Signify faced several challenges with their digital analytics implementation:

  • Inconsistent data collection across global markets
  • Need for enhanced privacy compliance amidst evolving regulations
  • Complex integration requirements with multiple external vendors
  • Limitations in marketing channel attribution accuracy
  • Balancing business analytics needs with privacy requirements

These challenges required a comprehensive solution that could standardize analytics while respecting regional privacy regulations.

The Solution

I developed and implemented a multi-faceted approach to address these challenges:

  • Created a custom Adobe setup framework that standardized data collection across all touchpoints
  • Implemented privacy-first design principles in all tracking implementations
  • Developed secure data collection methods for third-party vendors
  • Built a custom script for accurate marketing channel attribution
  • Established a cross-functional approach involving Legal and IT teams

This comprehensive solution ensured both analytical robustness and privacy compliance.
