The Challenge

AkzoNobel faced significant challenges in their global digital analytics infrastructure:

Complex Global Footprint

Managing 700+ websites across 40+ countries with varying regulatory requirements and technical implementations.

Evolving Privacy Landscape

Navigating the complex and constantly changing global privacy regulations including GDPR, CCPA, and other regional requirements.

Client-side Limitations

Traditional client-side tracking methods faced increasing challenges with browser restrictions and privacy controls.

Data Quality & Consistency

Maintaining consistent data collection and quality across a diverse range of websites, platforms, and markets.

The Solution

I developed a comprehensive approach to address these challenges:

Custom Server-side Framework

Designed and implemented a proprietary server-side measurement framework that processes data securely and with privacy controls built in.

Global Privacy Standard

Established a unified privacy standard that meets the requirements of all global regulations while providing flexibility for regional variations.

Cross-functional Collaboration

Worked closely with legal, IT, and marketing teams to ensure alignment and compliance throughout the implementation process.

Phased Implementation

Developed a strategic rollout plan to systematically implement the solution across all 700+ websites while minimizing disruption.
